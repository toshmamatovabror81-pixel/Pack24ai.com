## 2. Gofroqutilar — GOST 9142-2014 (9142-90), GOST 13511-2006
- Ijrolar: А — 4 klapanli, tutashuvchi tashqi klapanlar (RSC); Б/В — qisman/to'liq ustma-ust; Г, Д, Е, Ж, З (tubsiz); teleskopik, o'ramali, lotok, penal. Nisbat: L:B ≤2,5:1; H:B 0,5:1…2:1 (GOST 21140 moduli). [meganorm.ru]
- Ichki o'lcham chetlanishi: 2 mm (E/F gofr), 3 mm (Т, ДГС), 5 mm (П, С). Klapan tirqishi (зазор): ≤5 mm Т, ≤10 П, ≤14 С; vkladishli 20 mm; obechaykali 50 mm. Vysechka siljishi 5/10/14 mm; rilyovka perpendikulyarligi ≤8 mm/m. Tashqi balandlik = ichki + 1,0 sm (Т) / +2,0 sm (П); просечка 4–10 mm; biriktiruvchi klapan 20–50 mm; gofr yo'nalishi balandlikka parallel. [meganorm.ru]
- **BCT (усилие сопротивления сжатию), 9142-2014 4.1.4:** P = 9,8·K·K0·m·(n−1), N; m — brutto, kg; n — shtabeldagi qutilar soni; K = 0,6 (balandlik bo'ylab vysechkali) / 3,0 (shtabel yukini qabul qilmaydigan mahsulot); K0 = 1,86 (75–80 % namlik). Shtabel sinov yuki M = 0,1·Kу·P, kg, Kу = 0,82; 24 soat. 9142-90: K = 1,6 (saqlash ≤30 kun) / 1,65 (31–100 kun) / 1,85 (muddat yo'q); shtabel ≥250 sm; 30 min. [meganorm.ru]
- Karton tanlash (9142-90, 7-jadval, eng kichik o'lcham ≤125 mm): 7,5–10 kg — 1,1 MPa / 8 kN/m; 10–15 — 1,3/10; 15–20 — 1,5/12; 20–25 — 1,7/14; 25–40 kg — 2,0/16; o'lcham 326–350 mm: 7,5–20 kg — 0,7/4, 20–40 kg — 0,9/6; >40 kg — tajriba. Shtabel yukini qabul qilmaydigan mahsulot — ECT bo'yicha (6-jadval). ДГС-1/2/3: 780–1150 g/m², 4,0–5,0 mm, ECT ≥8,0/7,0/5,0 kN/m, teshilish ≥1,4/1,3/1,1 MPa. [meganorm.ru]
- Tashlash balandligi (brutto): 7,5–10 kg — 1000 mm; 10–15 — 800; 15–20 — 700; 20–25 — 650; 25–30 — 600; 30–35 — 550; 35–40 — 500 mm. [meganorm.ru]
- Skoba qadami ≤60 mm (≤10 kg), ≤45 (≤20 kg), ≤35 (>20 kg); sim 0,7–1,0 mm GOST 3282; yelim — PVA dispersiya GOST 18992. Klapanlar ≥10×180° bukish, yoriq ≤25 mm; dog' ≤20 mm; bosma siljishi ≤5 mm. Qabul: AQL 2,5 % (ko'rinish, o'lcham), 1,5 % (mustahkamlik); namuna 50 (≤1200 dona)…315 (>35 000). [meganorm.ru]
- GOST 13511-2006 (oziq-ovqat, gugurt, tamaki, yuvish vositalari): A ijro, 53 raqamli quti, Т11/Т12/Т22/Т23/Т24, chegaraviy massa 10–30 kg (№ 1 — 260×215×395 mm, 22,1 dm³, 0,628 m², 20 kg, Т11). Коробление ≤12 mm/m²; K = 1,65 (№ 11: 1,85), shtabel 2,5 m; ≥7 tashlash. [meganorm.ru]

## 4. Iste'mol qadog'i — GOST 12301-2006, 12303-80, 33781-2016

## Manbalar orasidagi ziddiyatlar
