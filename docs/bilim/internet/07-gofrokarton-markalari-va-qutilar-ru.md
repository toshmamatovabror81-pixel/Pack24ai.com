## 3. Layner va flyuting
- Kraftliner (крафтлайнер) — oqartirilmagan sulfat sellyuloza; testliner (тестлайнер) — makulatura (OCC), 125–350 g/m², testliner 1/2/3 [elar.usfeu.ru]; kraftliner ≥80 % bargli sellyuloza; 100 % sulfat = top-layner; testliner yuzasi yelimlanadi/kashirlanadi [kartonpak.ru]
- Layner gramaji: 125, 150, 175, 225 g/m²; eng qattiq 200–450 g/m² [korobok.ru]
- Flyuting (флютинг, бумага для гофрирования): 80–160 g/m², НСПЦ (NSSC) yoki makulatura; ≥150 g/m² da polusellyuloza ustun, 240 g/m² gacha; 15–30 % ignabargli sulfat [elar.usfeu.ru]; ko'p ishlatiladigan 112, 127, 150 g/m²; og'ir yuk 175, 200, 250 g/m² [korobok.ru]; yengil minimal 70–80 g/m² [elar.usfeu.ru]
- Testliner vs kraftliner: burst ~70 %, RCT ~90 %; Yevropa 70/30 %, kraftliner ~25 % qimmat [elar.usfeu.ru]; charchoq: testliner 180 g/m² 46 %, kraftliner 150 g/m² 70–80 % [arzpuck.ru]
- Layner kodlari: K (kraft), T2, T (100 % ikkilamchi), C (chip), BW, WT (white top), MK, OY; flyuting: SF, WS (Wellenstoff), WBF, Schrenz [kartonpak.ru]

## 5. Marka tanlash va hisob
- ГОСТ 9142-2014 usuli faqat uch qatlamli RSC uchun: P (BCT) = f(brutto m kg, qatlam n, K, K0, K1). K = 0,6 (vysechkali), 3,0 (shtabel), amalda 4,0; K0 = 1,86 (RH 75–80 %); K1 — geometriya, butun son, 6…20 [gofro.org]
- ECT — Д1-jadval (peregorodka bo'lsa pastki qiymat); marka — 3-jadval 2-sinf; shtabelsiz (konserva, shisha) — Д2-jadval: BST (MPa) / rilyovka (kN/m), 1-sinf [gofro.org]
- McKee: BCT = 5,87 × ECT × √(T × Z), T — qalinlik mm, Z — perimetr mm; umumiy BCT = K·ECT^a·FS^b·D^c [gofro.org; arzpuck.ru]
- ВНИИБ: BST ≈ 0,7 × ΣBST_qatlam; RCT: ECT = A × (RCT_l1 + K × RCT_fl + RCT_l2), A = 1, K = 1,32; Kotlas KK125–BK112–KK125: ECT 4,69 kN/m o'lchangan, 3,52 hisob → A ≈ 1,33; xossalar 5–15 % o'zgaradi, kiruvchi nazorat shart [gofro.org]
- BCT_talab = BCT₀ (23 °C, 50 % RH) × Cт, Cт = C1·C2·C3·C4·C5 = 2–7 (odatda 3–4); 200 kg lab → 50 kg; qo'lda yig'ilgan +25 % [arzpuck.ru]
- Zaxira: ustunli 1,00–1,33, «в замок» 1,67–2,00; RH 65 % 1,07–1,25, 75 % 1,25–1,67, 90 % 1,82–2,50; 90 % da 10 kun 1,33–2,00, 60 kun 2,00–2,55 [arzpuck.ru]
- Namlik: 310 → 120 kg; 350±50 → 130±15 kg (90 % RH, 72 h); vibratsiya −40 % [arzpuck.ru]; 09xx ajratgichlar BCT ni oshiradi [antech.ru]

## Manbalar orasidagi ziddiyatlar

- Sinf va shtabel yuki: korobok.ru 1-sinf (Т11–Т15, 20 kg gacha) ni shtabel yukini ko'taradigan, 2-sinf (Т21–Т27, 100 kg gacha) ni ko'tarmaydigan deb beradi; gofro.org (ГОСТ Р 52901-2007 va ГОСТ 9142-2014 bo'yicha) aksincha — 2-sinf shtabel yukini ko'taradi, 1-sinf ko'tarmaydi.
- 2-sinf markalar ro'yxati: gofro.org Т-21…Т-24; korobok.ru Т21–Т27.
- Kraftliner tola tarkibi: elar.usfeu.ru — oqartirilmagan sulfat (ignabargli) sellyuloza; kartonpak.ru — kamida 80 % bargli daraxt sellyulozasi.
- Flyuting gramaj diapazoni: elar.usfeu.ru 80–160 g/m² (polusellyulozali 240 g/m² gacha); korobok.ru 112–150 g/m² odatiy, 175–250 g/m² og'ir yuk uchun.
