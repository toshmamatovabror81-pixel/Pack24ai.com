# Narxlash, sifat va sinov standartlari (EN manbalar)

## 1. Narx formulasi va xarajat tarkibi
- Quti narxi = Karton tannarxi (board cost) + Konvertatsiya (converting) + Marja; Karton = Layner $/lb × og'irlik + Medium $/lb × og'irlik, birlik lb/MSF (MSF = 1 000 kv. fut) [corrugatednews.com]
- 32 ECT C-flute bir qavatli misol: 2 × 42 lb/MSF layner = 84 + medium 26 lb × 1,43 (take-up factor) = 37,2 → ≈121 lb/MSF; tipik narx $55–65/MSF [corrugatednews.com]
- Containerboard tayyor quti tannarxining 45–55% [corrugatednews.com]; qog'oz (layner + medium) 60–70% [corrugatedboxsuppliers.com]
- Konvertatsiya yalpi marjasi 25–35%, EBITDA 8–15%; xarajat tarkibi: ishchi kuchi 35–40%, bosma 15–25%, shtamp/asbob 5–15%, yelim 5–8%, amortizatsiya 10–15%, kommunal 5–8%, chiqindi 3–5%, overhead 10–15% [corrugatednews.com]
- Xomashyo +$50/t = $0,025/lb ≈ $0,009/quti (3 kv. fut, 121 lb/MSF); 10 mln RSC/yil → ≈$90 000/yil [corrugatednews.com]

## 6. FEFCO sinov metodlari (Testing Methods)
- Atmosfera: karton 23 ± 1 °C / 50 ± 2% RH (EN 20187); qutilar 23 ± 2 °C / 50 ± 3% RH [fefco.org]
| TM | Sinov | Parametr / ekvivalent |
|---|---|---|
| TM 1 | Namuna olish | n = ∛N: ≤1 000→10; ≤5 000→15; ≤10 000→20; ≤20 000→25; ≤30 000→30; >30 000→40 |
| TM 2 | Grammaj | G = g×10⁶/(a×b) g/m², 200×250 mm, ≥10 (= EN ISO 536) |
| TM 3 | Qalinlik (calliper) | 20 ± 0,5 kPa, 10 cm², 0,05 mm (= ISO 3034) |
| TM 4 | Bursting (Mullen) | halqa 31,5 mm, kPa (ISO 2759) |
| TM 5 | Puncture (PET) | J, 10/tomon (ISO 3036) |
| TM 6 | FCT | kPa, 12,5 mm/min (ISO 3035); double wall uchun yaroqsiz |
| TM 7 | Cobb 1800 | A = (m₂−m₁)/S g/m², 30 min (ISO 535) |
| TM 8 | ECT | R = F/L kN/m, 100×25 mm (= EN ISO 3037) |
| TM 11 | PAT | N/m, pin A 3 / B 2 / C 2,5 mm |
| TM 50 | BCT | N, 12,5 mm/min, pre-load 5% ≤200 N (ISO 12048; TAPPI T 804 ~5% past) |
| TM 51/52/53 | Drop / vibr 2–7 Hz / incline 10° | ISO 2248 / 2247 / 2244 |
- Qog'oz: SCT ISO 9895, RCT ISO 12192, CMT ISO 7263, namlik ISO 287 [fefco.org]
- Warp Factor (R 110, 2018): H (mm, 0,5 mm aniqlik) va L (mm) → 500 mm ga normallash; H=20/L=500 → WF 20 [fefco.org]

## 7. ISTA / ASTM transport sinovlari
- ISTA 3A (2018): ≤70 kg (150 lb) posilka; Small <13 000 cm³, ≤350 mm, ≤4,5 kg; Flat ≤200 mm; Elongated ≥900 mm; ketma-ketlik: TB1 kondisiya → TB3 9 tashlash → TB4/7 random vibration Grms 0,53/0,46 → TB9 8 tashlash → TB10 edge drop 200 mm → TB12/13 400 mm → TB14 leak 8 soat; dunnage C-flute burst 1 380 kPa yoki ECT 7,0 kN/m; basis weight o'zgarsa retest [ista.org]
- Uskuna: drop ISO 2248 / ASTM D5276, vibr ISO 13355 / D4728, past bosim ISO 2873 / D6653 (60 / 70 kPa) [ista.org]
- ASTM D4169: DC 1–18 (DC 13 ≤150 lb posilka, DC 6 pallet, DC 12 havo+unitized); AL I/II/III, AL II odatiy; mezon 1 mahsulot butun, 2 qadoq butun, 3 ikkalasi; kondisiyalash D4332 ≥72 soat; ASTM D7386 ≤68 kg yakka posilka [steris-ast.com]
- ISTA faqat quyi chegara beradi — over-packaging'ni aniqlamaydi; "reduce to damage" / "pass with minimum margin" [unifi.it]

## 8. Nuqsonlar va saqlash
- Warp (S, twist, down, up) — namlik farqi (differential moisture); 24–48 soat kondensatsiya; washboarding — ortiqcha yelim, A/C flute ko'proq, C→B→E kamaytiradi; delamination — green/dry/wet bond; flute crush — nip bosimi, caliper/ECT/FCT pasayadi [corrugatednews.com]
- Saqlash (PMMI B155-TR2.3-2018): 4–38 °C (40–100 °F), RH 30–70%; >70% — zaiflashish, yelim yumshash; <30% — mo'rtlik; FIFO, 3–6 oyda eng yaxshi yuradi; funksional muddat ≥12 oy; akklimatizatsiya ≥24 soat [fibrebox.org]

## 9. BCT va McKee
- McKee (1963): BCT = f(ECT, DMD/DCD bikrlik, P); faqat RSC, L < 3W, P < 7d; soddalashtirilgan (t) ortiqcha baholaydi; Batelka-Smith d hadi aniqlikni +68% / +42% [calpoly.edu]
- Bliss (FEFCO 0601): C-flute ECT 32 lb/in, caliper 3,97 mm, double-wall ECT 56 lb/in; ASTM D642-15, 1,3 cm/min, pre-load 22,68 kgf; BCT: S < SIF < SIFDC < V; CCF 5-down 60,96×40,64 / 10-down 40,64×30,48 cm, GMA pallet 40×48" [calpoly.edu]

Narx: "a typical 32 ECT C-flute board runs approximately $55-65 per MSF, varying by grade combination and purchase volume". - ✅ **tasdiqlandi** — 32→44 ECT ga o'tish karton tannarxini ≈30% oshiradi, 44→32 ECT ≈25% tejaydi; har o'lcham +1" → +5–10%; tirajni 2× oshirish dona narxini 8–15% kamaytiradi (setup 500 dona → $5,00/quti, 50 000 → $0,05) (corrugatednews.com)
