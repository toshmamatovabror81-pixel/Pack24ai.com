## 1. FEFCO kodi
- FEFCO Code — "Design Style Library for Corrugated Board Products", 12-nashr (2022), FEFCO (Bryussel), ICCA qabul qilgan; birinchi kod 1960-yillar. Gofrokarton qadoq konstruksiyalarini tasniflashning xalqaro tizimi; buyurtma va spetsifikatsiyada ishlatiladi [KW]

- Kod konstruksiyani bildiradi, o'lchamni emas: o'lcham, karton navi (board grade / марка картона), pardoz, toleranslar alohida kelishiladi [PD]

## 2. Yozuv qoidalari
- O'lcham: ichki (internal), mm, L x W x H; L — og'izdagi uzun tomon, W — qisqa (ba'zan W > L), H — og'izdan taggacha; o'zgaruvchan o'lcham "v" [KW]. Kite, IS 6481: L x B x H [KP; IS]; AQSh: L x W x D; tashqi o'lcham — palletlash uchun [DM; WP]
- O'lchash: 23 °C, 50 % RH (EN 20187), yassi zagotovkada bigiz markazidan, qalinlik hisobga olinadi [KW]
- 4-o'lcham: tashqi klapan overlap (o) — 355 x 205 x 120/40 mm; teleskopikda qopqoq balandligi H/h [KW]
- List: 1-o'lcham gofra bo'ylab, 2-o'lcham ko'ndalang [KW; 3M]
- Versiya: 0201.2 (nuqta); Kite eski yozuvda 0201-2 [KW]
- Birikma: 0201-G (glued) / 0201-S (stitched) [KW]. IS 6481: S/T/G, tutqich P/U; to'liq yozuv: "0320, Top 0207; Bottom 0204; S : P; Partitions 0933, 6 x 4" [IS]
- Kombinatsiya: ust/ost klapan — 0204/0215 [KW]
- Yig'ish: M (qo'lda), A (avtomat), M/A; Kite M+A ham [KP]. M/A: 0300, 0301, 0310, 0409, 0410, 0900; M: 0401, 0427, 0701, 0711 [KW]

## Tekshiruv (asosiy da'volar manbadan qayta o'qildi)

- ✅ **tasdiqlandi** — FEFCO 0201 (RSC): barcha klapanlar teng, tashqi klapan = 1/2 W, markazda uchrashadi; kesishda deyarli chiqindi yo'q — eng arzon va eng keng tarqalgan uslub; 0205 (CSO) uchun shart L ≤ 2 × W, 0206 ust-tagda 3 qavat karton beradi (packsize.com)
  - Sabab: Manba (packsize.com, "7 Variants on the Regular Slotted Container Box" blogi) da'voning barcha asosiy faktlarini tasdiqlaydi: 0201 (RSC) uchun "All flaps have the same length, and the two outer flaps are one-half the container's width", eng uzun klapanlar markazda uchrashadi, "This produces almost no waste when cut", va "0201, or the RSC, is the most common". 0205 (CSO) uchun: "this box's length can not be more than twice its width" (L ≤ 2W). 0206 uchun: "three full layers of corrugated cardboard protecting the top and bottom". Yagona nozik joy: "eng arzon" so'zi manbada to'g'ridan-to'g'ri yozilmagan — sahifa narx haqida gapirmaydi; packsize.com ning boshqa maqolasi ("Why the RSC is the top corrugated cardboard box") RSC ni "a very efficient design for large scale box manufacturing" va "almost no corrugated waste" deb ta'riflaydi, bu arzonlikni bilvosita qo'llab-quvvatlaydi, lekin zid kelmaydi.
- ✅ **tasdiqlandi** — FEFCO o'lchamlari ichki (internal), mm, L x W x H tartibida, 23 °C va 50 % RH (EN 20187) sharoitida yassi zagotovkada bigiz markazidan o'lchanadi; overlap to'rtinchi o'lcham sifatida yoziladi: 355 x 205 x 120/40 mm (kindermann-wellpappen.de)
  - Sabab: Manba: https://www.kindermann-wellpappen.de/downloads/2022-04-21_FEFCO%20Code_WEB_unprotected.pdf (FEFCO Code, saytda joylashtirilgan PDF). Hujjat quyidagilarni aynan bayon qiladi: "All dimensions are expressed as internal dimensions in mm as follows: Length (L) x Width (W) x Height (H)"; o'lchash sharoiti "standard climatic conditions (23°C and 50 % RH according to the standard EN 20187)"; o'lchash usuli "on the flat blank from the centre of crease bearing the thickness of the material in mind"; overlap haqida "the length of the area of overlapping (o) should be given as a fourth measurement after an oblique stroke", misol: "355 x 205 x 120/40 mm" (L) (W) (H) (o). Da'vodagi barcha elementlar (ichki o'lcham, mm, L x W x H, 23 °C / 50 % RH, EN 20187, yassi zagotovkada bigiz markazidan, overlap to'rtinchi o'lcham, 355 x 205 x 120/40 mm) manba bilan to'liq mos keladi.
- ✅ **tasdiqlandi** — Gofr profillari (3M RD-160.2): A 36±3 gofr/fut, balandlik 4,7 mm; B 50±3, 2,4 mm; C 42±3, 3,6 mm; E 94±3, 1,2 mm (laynersiz) (multimedia.3m.com)
  - Sabab: Manba (3M RD-160.2 "Corrugated Material and Component Construction", CGS 160, 4/3/2009, https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/296992O/cgs160-pkg-spec.pdf) bevosita o'qildi. "Corrugation Standard" jadvali aynan shunday: A — 36 ± 3 gofr/fut, 3/16 in (4,7 mm); B — 50 ± 3, 3/32 in (2,4 mm); C — 42 ± 3, 9/64 in (3,6 mm); E — 94 ± 3, 3/64 in (1,2 mm). Izoh B: "Height does not include thickness of facing" — bu "laynersiz" (qoplamasiz) balandlik degani; izoh A: qiymatlar taxminiy. Barcha raqamlar va shartlar mos.
- ✅ **tasdiqlandi** — RSC toleranslari (FBA/PMMI B155-TR2.1-2011): panel ±1/16" (1,5 mm), yassi varaq ±1/8" (3 mm); 3M: ichki o'lcham 76–610 mm uchun ±1,6 mm; warp ≤6,4 mm har 305 mm (douglas-machine.com)
  - Sabab: Manba (https://www.douglas-machine.com/standards-definitions/) FBA/PMMI B155-TR2.1-2011 ga tayanib aynan shu raqamlarni keltiradi: panel (bal chizig'idan bal chizig'igacha) ±1/16" (1,5 mm), yassi varaqning umumiy o'lchami ±1/8" (3 mm), ruxsat etilgan warp 12" (305 mm) da 1/4" (6,4 mm) [24"da 1/2", 36"da 3/4", 48"da 1"]. Bu raqamlar FBA saytidagi B155-TR2.1 (2018 nashri) matnida ham (5.1.1 va 5.3.1 bo'limlar) bir xil. 3M qismi Douglas sahifasida yo'q, lekin 3M ning o'z hujjati — Corporate General Specification RD-160.2 (multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/296992O/cgs160-pkg-spec.pdf) — oddiy (slotted) qutilar uchun ichki o'lcham 3,000–24,000" (76–610 mm) oralig'ida ±0,062" (≈±1,6 mm), 24" dan katta yoki 3" dan kichik bo'lsa ±0,094" (2,4 mm), warp esa 1 futda 0,250" (6,4 mm/305 mm) dan oshmasligini belgilaydi. Shunday qilib da'vo to'liq tasdiqlanadi; faqat eslatma: 3M figurasi douglas-machine.com da emas, 3M RD-160.2 da, va ±1/16" ni Douglas 1,5 mm, 3M 1,6 mm deb yaxlitlaydi (aniq 1,59 mm).
- ❌ **rad etildi** — Yelimlangan birikma: lap kengligi 31,75–38,1 mm, sovuq yelim ≥85 % / hot-melt ≥25 % qoplama, ajratganda ≥90 % fiber tear, aks holda jiddiy nuqson; sinov: burst TAPPI T 810, ECT TAPPI T 811 (referee) va T 839 (clamp) (multimedia.3m.com)
  - Sabab: Manba topildi va to'liq o'qildi: 3M "CGS 160 Corrugated Material and Component Construction Requirement" (RD-160.2, 4/3/2009, 17 bet, https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/296992O/cgs160-pkg-spec.pdf). "Glued Joint Requirements" bo'limi da'voning asosiy qismini aynan tasdiqlaydi: lap kengligi 1.250"–1.500" (= 31,75–38,1 mm, manbada faqat dyuymda); sovuq suyuq yelim qoplamasi lap maydonining 85 %; hot-melt qoplamasi kontakt maydonining 25 %; ajratganda kontakt maydonining kamida 90 % ida tola (fiber) ko'rinishi shart; "90 % dan kam yopishgan yelimlangan birikma" nuqsonlar jadvalida SERIOUS (rad etiladi/qaytariladi) deb tasniflangan. "Board Strength" bo'limi: burst — TAPPI T-810 (Mullen/Cady), ECT — TAPPI T-811. BIROQ hujjatda TAPPI T 839, "clamp" va "referee" so'zlari umuman yo'q (ikki marta maqsadli qidirildi, hujjat "End of Document" bilan to'liq). Hujjatda uchraydigan TAPPI usullari faqat T-810, T-811 va T-803 (uch qavatli karton uchun puncture). Shu sababli "T 811 (referee) va T 839 (clamp)" qismi 3M manbasiga noto'g'ri nisbat berilgan — bu TAPPI ning umumiy tasnifi bo'lishi mumkin, lekin 3M spetsifikatsiyasi aytmaydi. Da'vo qisman rad etildi: faqat ECT usuli qismi tuzatilishi kerak, qolgan raqamlar to'g'ri.
  - **Tuzatish:** Yelimlangan birikma (3M CGS 160, RD-160.2, 2009): lap kengligi 1,250–1,500 dyuym (31,75–38,1 mm); sovuq suyuq yelim qoplamasi lap maydonining 85 %, hot-melt qoplamasi kontakt maydonining 25 %; ajratganda kontakt maydonining kamida 90 % ida tola (fiber tear) ko'rinishi shart, 90 % dan kam yopishgan birikma — SERIOUS (jiddiy) nuqson. Sinov usullari (3M bo'yicha): burst — TAPPI T-810 (Mullen/Cady), ECT — TAPPI T-811 (uch qavatli karton uchun burst o'rniga puncture TAPPI T-803). 3M hujjati TAPPI T 839 (clamp) usulini va "referee" atamasini keltirmaydi; agar T 839 eslatilsa, uni 3M ga emas, TAPPI ga nisbat berish kerak.
